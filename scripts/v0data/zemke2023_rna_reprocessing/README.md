# Zemke 2023 RNA reprocessing

The published Zemke 2023 RNA BigWigs provide measured coordinate-resolved reads-per-kilobase-per-million targets for 20 subclasses in each of four species. The release also provides raw integer unique-molecular-identifier count matrices and cell metadata. Direct gene supervision is derived from those counts without replacing or rescaling the published tracks.

`download_annotations.sh` downloads National Center for Biotechnology Information Reference Sequence, NCBI RefSeq, exon annotations from the University of California Santa Cruz, UCSC, browser for the exact `hg38`, `rheMac10`, `calJac4`, and `mm10` assemblies used by the target BigWigs. `prepare_gene_supervision.py` sums raw counts by subclass, excludes the unpublished chandelier-cell class, normalizes each observed subclass pseudobulk to counts per million, maps gene symbols to native-assembly union exons, and writes the standard compact gene-supervision artifact. The released matrices contain `L4/5 IT` but lack the finer `L5 IT` assignment used by a separate published track in every species. The direct-gene artifact therefore preserves all 20 published channels and explicitly marks `L5_IT` invalid for count-derived loss and metrics; its measured coordinate track remains active.

The derived manifest preserves the published RNA tracks, resolutions, scaling, and coordinate-level loss. It adds direct gene-level log-count supervision with equal outer weight and retains coordinate coverage weight one. An optional signed double-centered correlation weight applies to direct gene expression because the training contract prefers measured gene targets whenever they are present. This is an optimization variant and does not replace the canonical published-track comparison.

`audit_gene_track_agreement.py` compares raw UMI CPM with exon-integrated published RPKM. It reports the requested raw and log1p double-centered correlations plus equal-gene-weight within-gene cell-group correlations. The raw CPM gate remains the compatibility criterion; within-gene values diagnose whether a failure reflects cell-group mismatch or gene-specific measurement scale and do not override that gate.

`prepare_published_gene_supervision.py` defines a separate objective after a raw-CPM compatibility failure. It integrates each published RNA track over native-assembly union exons, normalizes the modeled gene totals to CPM within each group, and attaches all 20 groups as direct-gene supervision while retaining coordinate coverage loss. `submit_zemke2023_published_gene_screen.sh` smoke-gates the human LoRA versus LoRA plus LoCon screen and then reevaluates each checkpoint with the canonical coordinate-only manifest. Direct-gene and coordinate metrics are therefore both required before this objective can be considered useful.

`audit_gene_track_agreement.py` integrates each published RPKM track over a deterministic sample of native-assembly union exons and divides by 1,000 to recover CPM scale. It compares those values with the raw-count pseudobulk using signed double-centered Pearson correlation over genes and subclasses. The audit verifies channel order and supervision dimensions and fails below a configurable agreement threshold, preventing a conflicting gene matrix from advancing to model training.

`audit_donor_reliability.py` uses the release's `orig.ident` labels to aggregate the same raw unique molecular identifier counts separately by donor and valid subclass. It restricts the count matrix to the exact modeled genes and excludes subclasses masked from direct supervision. Donors are assigned independently within each subclass to library-depth-balanced halves, each half is normalized to counts per million, and reliability is measured by signed double-centered correlation. The Spearman-Brown correction estimates reliability of the complete pseudobulk; its square root is an assumption-based model-correlation ceiling rather than an observed model result.

The published-track-integrated human target has 15,033 training genes and a median of five genes per sequence batch. Row-centering has cosine 0.99993 with split-wide double-centering. Gene-aware ordering reduces batches without genes from 3.6% to zero and batches without local double-centered variance from 8.4% to 0.3%. `../submit_zemke2023_published_row_correlation_screen.sh` therefore compares row-correlation weights 0, 1, and 10 under matched ordering, retaining both coordinate coverage and direct-gene mean-squared-error terms. Each resulting checkpoint is evaluated without updates against the canonical coordinate-only target before selection.

After all four species pass, `submit_zemke2023_direct_gene_screen.sh` runs a matched human LoRA and LoRA plus LoCon screen. It retains both published coordinate losses, adds direct RNA gene supervision, and applies signed double-centered correlation weight 10 to both heads, matching the strongest completed human Zemke objective screen. The launcher validates the complete four-species audit before submitting either smoke task.

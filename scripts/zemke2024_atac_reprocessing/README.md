# Zemke 2024 ATAC reprocessing

This directory reconstructs broad-subclass ATAC coverage targets from the indexed donor fragment resources. The metadata labels 18 broad subclasses, whereas the released coordinate tracks have 22 channels because Astro and microglia subclasses are split further. The reconstruction therefore starts as a separate 18-channel target family rather than silently substituting an incompatible signal for the released 22-channel head.

Each retained metadata cell contributes every recorded fragment and its multiplicity. Fragment coverage is accumulated in 100 bp bins after standard Tn5 end shifts, then normalized to signal per million fragments, SPMR, using each broad subclass's whole-genome fragment total. The one-donor chromosome 9 smoke run verifies barcode matching and target scale before an all-donor reaggregation and released-track comparison.

`audit_metadata_coverage.py` first measures retained-cell and retained-fragment overlap for every donor. It gates a full reaggregation: every donor must have complete metadata-cell coverage and the retained-fragment fraction must be reported rather than treated as an implicit downsampling rate.

`compare_released.py` compares either reconstructed broad-subclass coverage or Tn5 insertion SPMR to the matching released broad BigWigs at the native 100 bp bin size. It reports ordinary and signed double-centered correlation across tracks and genomic bins, plus per-subclass signal scale and correlation. This is a diagnostic, not an exact reproduction gate: the authors generated the released BigWigs from peak-filtered cell-type BED files with `bamCoverage --normalizeUsing RPKM`, while this pipeline retains all metadata-matched quality-controlled fragments and emits coverage or insertion SPMR. The peak-filtered BED inputs are not part of the local resource.

`audit_split_half.py` assesses whether the all-fragment coverage target is reproducible. It assigns whole cells, rather than individual fragments, into deterministic depth-balanced halves within each broad subclass. It then rebuilds both coverage-SPMR pseudobulks on one chromosome and reports signed double-centered split-half R, Spearman-Brown full-target reliability, and the square-root model-correlation ceiling. This measures variation between independently sampled cell collections without creating a deceptively easy technical fragment split.

`compose_raw_atac_targets.py` replaces only the published ATAC head in the established direct-gene target manifest. The resulting variant has 18 raw broad-subclass ATAC channels with materializer-derived nonzero means and retains the direct raw-UMI CPM RNA head, including its existing missing-subtype mask and gene-supervision layout.

`slurm_full_chromosome_array.sbatch` reconstructs canonical autosomes and chromosome X without downsampling. `slurm_materialize_and_compose.sbatch` then materializes the coverage shards with the existing generic BigWig writer and writes the composed joint target manifest. The split-half audit gates adopting this new target for training, not the reproducible preprocessing itself. Mitochondrial and Y chromosomes are intentionally omitted to match the established human joint-training split policy.
